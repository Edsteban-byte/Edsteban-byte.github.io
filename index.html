My Story

It started with a question: How can we make systems more efficient?

My journey began in Business Administration, where I earned my DEUG with a specialization in Administrative & Economic Governance. That education taught me the logic of business—how moving parts fit together to create value. But I realized that in the modern world, the most powerful tool to drive that value is Technology.

Now, I combine that structural knowledge with front-end development to build applications that aren't just pretty, but robust and secure.

Currently based in Sunrise, FL, I apply my troubleshooting skills daily as an Advisor, while spending my nights coding and finding ways to make systems more secure.
